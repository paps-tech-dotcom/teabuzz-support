TeaBuzz Support

Need help with TeaBuzz? We're here to help with your account, technical issues, reports, and other questions.

Contact Support

For help with TeaBuzz, please contact our support team by email. Email is the primary support channel for TeaBuzz.

blackishtechnologies@gmail.com

When contacting us, please describe the issue you're experiencing and include any information that may help us understand and resolve the problem.

What Can We Help With?

  • Account and sign-in problems
  • Profile and account settings
  • Posts, comments, reactions, and community features
  • Confessions and polls
  • Notifications and app functionality
  • Marketplace-related questions
  • Technical problems or app errors
  • Reporting inappropriate content or behavior
  • Privacy and account-related requests
  • Other questions about TeaBuzz

Reporting a Problem

If you encounter a technical problem, please email our support team with as much information as possible.

Helpful information may include:

  • A description of what happened
  • The steps that caused the problem
  • Your TeaBuzz username, if relevant
  • Your device model
  • Your operating system version
  • The version of the TeaBuzz app you're using
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if helpful

Account & Privacy Requests

If you have a question about your account or want to make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the support email above.

Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive account credentials in your email.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact TeaBuzz support?

Email us at blackishtechnologies@gmail.com .

What information should I include in a support email?

Tell us what happened, what you were trying to do, and any relevant device or app information. Screenshots can also help us investigate an issue.

Can I report inappropriate content?

Yes. Please contact support with details about the content or behavior you would like us to review.

Can I ask about my account or privacy?

Yes. Contact us by email and explain your request. We may ask for additional information to verify the request.
